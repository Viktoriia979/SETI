SETI

SETI stands for Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence. It is a scientific effort to search for signs of intelligent life beyond Earth. Instead of looking for tiny organisms, SETI mainly looks for evidence that an advanced civilisation might exist somewhere in space.

SETI scientists use radio telescopes and other instruments to listen for unusual signals coming from space. They search for signals that seem too organised or unusual to have been created naturally. Computers help scientists examine huge amounts of data and identify signals that may be worth investigating further.

Scientists have detected some interesting signals over the years, but none have been confirmed as messages from extraterrestrial life. One famous example was the Wow! signal, detected in 1977. It was a strong, unusual radio signal that lasted about 72 seconds, but scientists have never been able to prove where it came from. The search for intelligent life continues today.
